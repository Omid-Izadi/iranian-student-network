What the network does

Iranian student clubs do great work, but most of them work alone and start over every year when their board graduates. The network gives them a shared place to begin from.

A directory of every group

One list of Iranian and Persian student clubs, so students can find the group at their school and groups can find each other.

A shared calendar

Nowruz, Yalda, talks and meetups across campuses, so nearby groups can join each other's events instead of competing for the same night.

Speakers and mentors

A speaker who visits one campus can visit three. Alumni can mentor students wherever they study.

Help starting a group

If your school has no Iranian club yet, we'll share what other founders learned and connect you with them.

Open to every Iranian student

Whatever your background, language, religion or politics, and whether you were born in Iran or not, this network is yours.

We don't speak for any government or party, and we don't take sides in politics. Clubs decide their own views.

We list only what clubs publish about themselves. We never share members' personal information.
